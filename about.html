About

I'm from China, currently an undergraduate in the Department of Life Science and Technology, School of Life Science and Technology, Institute of Science Tokyo, expecting to graduate in March 2029.

I'm compiling a handbook of undergraduate mathematics covering topics such as calculus, linear algebra, complex numbers, probability and statistics, and differential equations, which serves as both my study notes and an ongoing personal project. This site documents my learning process, and I'm open to conversations in related fields.
